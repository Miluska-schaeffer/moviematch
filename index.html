Key decisions

  1. Interviews

    3 of 5 rejected films for mood or length, not taste

    Changed

    Mood and runtime filter the deck before anyone swipes

  2. Lo-fi test

    2 of 4 testers said it felt like a scheduling tool

    Changed

    Playful copy and mood-themed visuals

  3. Hi-fi data check

    9 of 30 films pulled weren’t on any streaming service

    Changed

    Only films on the group’s chosen services

Behind the project

The problem

Picking a movie as a group takes 10–40 minutes.

  • Mood and length decide, not taste
  • Hardest for 4+ people
  • Nobody wants an install

Based on 10 survey responses, 5 interviews and a lo-fi usability test with 4 people. Hi-fi testing is next.
